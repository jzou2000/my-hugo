### image

In the field of image generation, the most famous closed-source commercial models include Midjourney, DALL-E 3 by OpenAI, and Ideogram, which are known for their exceptional artistic quality and prompt adherence.

在图像生成领域，最著名的闭源商业模型包括 Midjourney、OpenAI 的 DALL-E 3 以及 Ideogram，它们以卓越的艺术质量和提示词遵循能力而闻名。

On the open-source side, [FLUX](https://bfl.ai/) by Black Forest Labs (founded by the original creators of Stable Diffusion) is currently one of the most powerful and popular open-weight models.

在开源方面，由 Black Forest Labs（由 Stable Diffusion 的原始创建者创立）推出的 FLUX 是目前最强大、最受欢迎的开源权重模型之一。

Other notable open-source image models include the Stable Diffusion series (like SDXL and SD3) by Stability AI, Kolors by Kuaishou, and Hunyuan-DiT by Tencent.

其他值得注意的开源图像模型包括 Stability AI 的 Stable Diffusion 系列（如 SDXL 和 SD3）、快手的 Kolors（可图）以及腾讯的 Hunyuan-DiT。

### video

For video generation, OpenAI's Sora is the most discussed closed-source model, alongside highly capable commercial platforms like Runway Gen-3 Alpha, Luma Dream Machine, and Pika.

在视频生成方面，OpenAI 的 Sora 是讨论度最高的闭源模型，此外还有 Runway Gen-3 Alpha、Luma Dream Machine 和 Pika 等功能强大的商业平台。

In the Chinese market, Kuaishou's Kling (可灵) and MiniMax's Hailuo (海螺AI) are considered top-tier commercial video models, competing directly with global leaders.

在中国市场，快手的可灵 (Kling) 和 MiniMax 的海螺AI (Hailuo) 被视为顶级的商业视频模型，直接与全球领先者竞争。

Regarding open-source video models, Tencent's HunyuanVideo, Zhipu AI's CogVideoX, Genmo's Mochi 1, and Lightricks' [LTX-Video](https://ltx.io/studio) are currently the leading choices for developers and researchers.

在开源视频模型方面，腾讯的 HunyuanVideo（混元视频）、智谱 AI 的 CogVideoX、Genmo 的 Mochi 1 以及 Lightricks 的 LTX-Video 是目前开发者和研究人员的领先选择。
